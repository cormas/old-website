Getting ready to use Cormas



CORMAS has been developped with the non-commercial version of VisualWorks® from Cincom Systems, which is therefore needed to be installed on your computer before to get ready to use Cormas.

The latest version of Cormas was released in February 2007.

  • For a first installation, when you have never installed VisualWorks on your computer before, please follow these steps:
    1. Get the latest non-commercial version of VisualWorks from the Cincom website (the instructions to complete this step are detailed here).
    2. Install VisualWorks on your computer (the instructions to complete this step are detailed here)
    3. Get the latest version of Cormas (download this file: cormas.zip)
    4. Install Cormas on your computer (the instructions to complete this step are detailed here)
  • If Cormas is already installed on your computer, but the VisualWorks version is out of date* (prior to 7.4), your cormas is also for sure out of date! Please follow these steps:
    1. Be sure to keep the existing Cormas models by moving the whole "Models" subdirectory of Cormas on your desktop
    2. Remove the whole VisualWorks directory that was installed on your computer (beware to keep a copy of the Cormas models before, see just above!)
    3. Get the latest non-commercial version of VisualWorks from the Cincom website (the instructions to complete this step are detailed here).
    4. Install VisualWorks on your computer (the instructions to complete this step are detailed here)
    5. Get the latest version of Cormas (download this file: cormas.zip)
    6. Install Cormas on your computer (the instructions to complete this step are detailed here)
  • If you already have an up-to-date version of VisualWorks installed on your computer (later than 7.4), but your Cormas version is out-of-date**, you just need to update Cormas:
    1. Get the latest version of Cormas (download this file: cormas.zip), and overwrite existing files when unzipping it.
    2. Update Cormas on your computer (the instructions to complete this step are detailed here)

* Click here to know how to check the version of VisualWorks that is currently installed on your computer
** Click here to know how to check the version of Cormas that is currently installed on your computer
