What management tools in Niger
 Regulatory and economic tools :
Zoning and global quotas
differential tax (based on zone and distance to urban areas)
Rural markets : a new oragnization of the exploitation and the trade of fuelwood
Law :
Transfer of fuelwood use rights from the state to rural villages.

Issue
The analysis of the efficiency of management tools of renewable resource exploitation
Political measures / collective normes comparison of several scenarios
Analysis of the effects according to the actual level of application

Slide 119

Behavioral rules
for the consumer agent
synthesis of X urban inhabitants
represented as a demand function
data from the field to set initial price, max. quantity, max. price (price of an energy substitute)
it receives the quantities from the traders, then updates its price

Behavioral rules
for the trader agent
each one has a specific transport capacity
each one has a max. cost and a min. quantity for transporting wood
each one is requesting for quantities at a given price to a harvester, according to the price of the market, its capacities and costs (offer function)

Behavioral rules
for the harvester agent
they are spatially located
they are individually contacted by a trader
they have a specific reserve threshold  and a specific cost for harvesting that depends on the distance
they decide to harvest or not, depending on the proposed price
they increase progressively the distance when collecting
they follow the quotas of the zone

Simulations : 3 scenarios
Reference simulation : no tools, no zoning
Simulation with zoning and quotas
3 zones with specific quotas and rules
global quotas at the level of the harvesters
Simulation with wood taxation
two levels of taxes according to the distance to the market
fixed taxe for a given volume bought by any trader

Assessment criteria
Duration of the simulation experiments
30 years
yearly time-step
Assessment criteria
ecological : biomass evolution for dead and green wood
the quantities exchanged by the actors
 prices and margins : who is winning and who is losing ?
