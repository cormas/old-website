Scientific Approach

General framework

At CIRAD, our research on modelling has been developed within the Green Research Unit (Renewable Resource Management, Environment), which focuses on interactions between resources and societies. Our Research Unit is part of CIRADs Environments and Societies Department. Multi-agent systems (MAS) are simulation tools that are particularly well suited to studying the dynamics of interactions between resources and societies. Our approach to modelling and managing natural resources has been defined and refined in interaction with other schools of thought. These include ecology. In addition, two approaches to resource management have enriched our thinking: economic ecology and the so-called common pool resource approach. These schools of thought use different types of models and simulations. We combine the development of these models with an approach based on the use of modelling.
